# Brand: Hamed, and Fair and Square

Drafts, 2026-10-22. Each part stands alone: change one, and the others still hold. Words live where the page
reads them (about.html, stage/words.en.ts); this file says what they are for.

Goals, in order: contracts and jobs > shares > gifts. Visitors should get there by their own reasoning: no
nagging, and no questions put to them.

**Bio, short (≈30 words).** Hamed Seyed-Allaei is a physicist (PhD, SISSA) who builds models you can play with,
and helps people test and improve theirs. Ten years of production machine learning at Zalando and Delivery Hero.

**Bio, long (≈60 words).** Hamed Seyed-Allaei is a physicist. In 2006 he published on extremely skewed networks;
twenty years later he built Fair and Square, a playable version of the yard-sale model, where fair trades pile the
money onto one. Between the two: a PhD at SISSA, university teaching, and ten years of production machine learning
at Zalando and Delivery Hero. Check against his CV before use.

**Voice.**

- Plain, short sentences, first person. Facts with numbers; no adjectives about himself (Ogilvy).
- Show, never claim: the paper, the formula, the working yard. Never "expert", "passionate", "world-class",
  "unique", "innovative".
- Diagnose, don't pitch (Enns): name situations the reader can recognise, including "I can't name the problem
  yet". Don't accept a client's diagnosis before looking. Never beg, never apologise for selling, never a sob story.
- Credit generously and by name (Kleon). Say what came from whom.
- Warm and a little funny about himself and the squares; serious about the maths.
- English that survives translation into Persian: few idioms, no puns that the meaning depends on.

## 2. Fair and Square

**Promise.** A fair game you can check yourself. What it does is yours to find.

**Lines it can use.** "Same coin, same rules." · "Nobody cheats." · "We play fair." · #FairAndSquare

**Voice.** The yard explains; the words only name what was seen. Questions, not conclusions. Readable by a
ten-year-old, and still true for a physicist. Claims stay inside the model ("in this yard", never "in the
economy").

**Visual rules.**

- Type: Gelasio (serif) for character: the wordmark, the tabs, people's names, headings of things. Vazirmatn for
  everything read: prose, the panel, labels, numbers.
- Lockup: "fair &" and a square, cap-height, in place of the word "square". The square has a face (in the stage
  it follows the Gini). The lockup is always in English. Favicon: one square, black and white, normal face.
- Colour: paper `#f4efe4`, ink `#28251f`, muted `#7a7262`, ampersand `#958c7b`, active brown `#4d271c`, glow
  `#e9b949`. Squares wear their own colours. Hamed is the blue square everywhere. Highlights are in the square's
  own colour.
- Shape: everything square: buttons, slider thumbs, frames, plots. Square corners on buttons.
- Characters: only the stage's own faces in the game. (A labelled process archive may show original drawings.)
- Layout: logical CSS only (`inline`/`block`), one `dir` per locale.

**Honesty (the brand is "not cheating").** No fake scarcity or timers, no invented numbers, no testimonials
nobody gave, no pop-ups, no pre-ticked boxes, no guilt. A shared line carries only the yard's own numbers.
Affiliate links say so. AI help is credited.
