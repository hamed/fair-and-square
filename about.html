The model is not mine: Anirban Chakraborti wrote it down and Brian Hayes named it the yard-sale model, both in 2002; Bruce Boghosian and colleagues worked out its equations (2014, 2015); Christoph Börgers and Claude Greengard proved that, under its basic rules, one share approaches everything. Learn's last question, choosing a yard before you know which square you'll be, is John Rawls's veil of ignorance. The faces' moods sit on James Russell's map of feelings. The letters are Gelasio, by Eben Sorkin, and Vazirmatn, by Saber Rastikerdar; Build's blocks are Blockly. This game's pictures, play and code are mine, written with help from AI coding assistants.
